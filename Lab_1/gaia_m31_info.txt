GAIA M31-FIELD LIGHT CURVES -- GU4303 linear-regression lab
============================================================
File: gaia_m31_lcs.csv (one row per photometric epoch)
  source_id  Gaia DR3 source identifier (group rows by this)
  t_year     observation epoch, calendar years (2014.6 - 2017.4)
  gmag       Gaia G-band magnitude at that epoch
  gmag_err   photon-noise uncertainty on gmag (magnitudes)

~2,000 stars, G < 17, >= 30 epochs each, from the Gaia DR3 "GAPS" field
centred on M31 (Evans et al. 2023, A&A 674, A4) -- the only Gaia field whose
time-series were released for EVERY source rather than only for stars
pre-selected as variable. At these magnitudes the sample is dominated by
ordinary Milky Way foreground stars.

Remember: magnitudes run backwards. A POSITIVE slope dm/dt means the star is
getting FAINTER (dimming); negative means brightening.
